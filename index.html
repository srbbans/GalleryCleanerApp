Questions

Good things to ask before you install.

Does it upload my photos anywhere?

No. The app has no permission to use the internet, so Android won't let it connect to anything. Every photo is analyzed on your phone and stays there.

What if I remove something by mistake?

Removed items go to your phone's trash, not straight to deletion. You can restore them from “Recently deleted” in the app, or from your gallery's trash, for about 30 days.

Will it touch my favorite photos?

Photos you've marked as favorites in your gallery are never pre-selected. You can also mark any photo as “Keep” in Gallery Cleaner, and it won't be suggested again.

How does it know which photo is the best one?

For a group of similar shots it compares sharpness, brightness and whether anyone's eyes are closed, then suggests the one that came out best. You can always pick a different one.

Can I choose just a few photos to share with it?

Yes. On Android 14 and newer you can allow access to selected photos only. Results will cover just those, and you can allow the rest later.

Will it slow down or drain my phone?

The first scan runs in the background with a progress notification. After that, only new photos are checked. You can also tell it to do its heavier work only while charging.

Which phones does it work on?

Any phone running Android 11 or newer.
